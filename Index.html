The Idea

Food is how people connect.

Spicy Studio started as a simple question: what if a cloud kitchen was also a living, breathing community space where people actually show up?

On Swiggy and Zomato, we're a kitchen. But behind that, we're building something rarer — an open, warm, creative space in Bangalore where food is just the beginning.

After-work hangouts. Weekend cooking sessions. Popups. Late-night experiments. Content creation. People who just needed somewhere to be. That's the real Spicy Studio.
